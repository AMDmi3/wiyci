The World Is Your CI (beta)

wiyci aggregates downstream build logs of Free and Open Source software projects and presents them as a typical Continuous Integration platform would, highlighting compiler warnings and failed tests. We hope to increase upstream awareness of downstream build problems and encourage the community to contribute fixes.

wiyci currently sources build logs from Alpine, Fedora, Nix, and FreeBSD, covering a variety of kernels (Linux, FreeBSD, Darwin), system libraries (glibc, musl, FreeBSD libc, Apple libc), hardware architectures (x86_64, x86, arm, aarch64, loongarch64, ppc64, riscv64, s390x), and numerous{# may change to "countless" as we grow #} toolchains.

Feel free to suggest more build log sources, more facts to parse, or projects not yet covered that you're interested in.
